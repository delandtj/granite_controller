# PM-19V-250W-B port module (LM5116, single phase) - component spec

**Date**: 2026-10-30
**Status**: Draft, schematic and board in `power/pm-19v-250w-b/`
**Replaces nothing**: PM-19V-250W rev A (`power/pm-19v-250w/`, LTC7810 two
phase) stays as it is. B is a cheaper module with the same function and
the same mechanical and electrical interface; a host cannot tell them
apart except by measurement.

Inputs: docs/plane-requirements.md (R1-R22), docs/pm-interface.md (outline,
screws, aux header, ID table, heights), docs/adr/0003 (module decisions).
Controller datasheet: TI LM5116 SNVS499I (Nov 2023); equation numbers
below are that datasheet's ("eq 37" = its equation 37).

## 1. What it is

A non-isolated synchronous buck, 38-66 V in, 19.0 V / 13.2 A out, on the
58.4 x 22.9 mm module outline: one LM5116 controller, one high-side and one
low-side 100 V MOSFET, one 10 uH inductor, a sense resistor in the low-side
source, ceramic input caps, ceramic plus solid-polymer output caps. Around it,
unchanged in function from rev A: OV_KILL and PGOOD comparators, ID
resistor, RUN, VIN UVLO and OVLO, the four screw pads and the 2x3 aux header.

## 4. Design calculations

Symbols as in the datasheet: A = 10 V/V (current sense gain), gm = 5 uA/V
(ramp transconductance), RS = sense resistor, T = 1/fsw.

### 4.1 Switching frequency (eq 1)

RT = (T - 450 ns) / 284 pF. RT = 12.4k gives T = 12.4k x 284p + 450n =
3.97 us, fsw = 252 kHz.

Why 252 kHz: the loss estimate (section 4.10) is flat between 200 and
300 kHz (7.5 W at 200 kHz, 7.7 W at 250 kHz, 8.0 W at 300 kHz, 48 V full
load). Higher fsw costs high-side switching and Qrr loss; lower fsw needs
more inductance for the same ripple (a 1510-size 15 uH part has 11.5 mOhm,
+0.5 W copper) and lets a metal-dust core run at larger flux swing. 252 kHz
with 10 uH gives 30-40 % ripple, a crossover near 20 kHz, and a min on-time
margin of 11x (1.15 us at 66 V against 100 ns). The hiccup timer note
(datasheet 6.3.3: with no UVLO cap above ~250 kHz the hiccup is effectively
disabled) does not apply: CFT is fitted.

### 4.2 Inductor (eq 8)

IPP = VOUT (1 - VOUT/VIN) / (L fsw):

| VIN | 38 V | 48 V | 60 V | 66 V |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPP (10 uH) | 3.8 A (29 %) | 4.6 A (35 %) | 5.2 A (39 %) | 5.4 A (41 %) |
| Ipeak at 13.2 A | 15.1 A | 15.5 A | 15.8 A | 15.9 A |

COILANK APS1510M100F (C49261402): 10 uH +/-20 %, DCR 8.6 mOhm max, Irms 22 A,
Isat 26.5 A (COILANK defines Isat at ~40 % drop), 16.5 x 15.5 x 10.0 mm,
shielded metal-dust, same land as the Coilcraft XAL1510 (KiCad
L_Coilcraft_XAL1510-103; the APS1510 fit check against this land is in
docs/handoff.md, 2026-10-27). Drop-in second source on the same land:
cjiang FEXL1510A-100M (C5378597, 727 stock, $3.66, Isat 29 A at 30 % drop;
but rated 30 V DC absolute, see risk 4). Copper loss 13.2^2 x 8.6m = 1.5 W
(1.8 W hot). Height 10.0 mm on F, under the 12 mm limit.

Taller or lower-DCR parts were checked and do not fit: the flat-wire 10 uH
parts with 2.3-2.9 mOhm (FC-SE2918-100M C3037600, MTP2918E-100M-H
C20069087) are 18 mm tall; APS1513M100F (7 mOhm) is 13 mm; 22 x 22 mm
parts do not fit the 22.9 mm board width.

### 4.3 Current sense resistor, ramp, current limit (eq 2-5, 37-39)

"Best performance method" (eq 37): IOS = (VOUT / 3) x 10 uA/V = 63.3 uA,
which makes the DC current limit independent of VIN:

ILIM(dc) = VCS(TH) / RS - VOUT T / L

VCS(TH) = 110 mV typ (94-126 mV, VCC > 5.8 V; with VCCX powered and
4.5 < VCC < 5.8 V the comparator reference rises to 1.72 V, eq 6, only
during start-up). With RS = 4.0 mOhm: ILIM = 27.5 - 7.55 = **19.95 A
typical**, 15.95 A at 94 mV, 23.95 A at 126 mV. Peak inductor current at the
limit adds IPP/2 (2.7 A at 66 V): 22.6 A typ, 26.6 A worst case.

RS: Liang RLP25FEGR004 (C459681), 4 mOhm 1 % 2512, 3 W. Loss (1 - D) x
13.2^2 x 4m = 0.42 W at 48 V, 0.50 W at 66 V.

CRAMP = IOS L / (VOUT A RS) = 63.3u x 10u / (19 x 10 x 0.004) = 833 pF ->
**820 pF C0G 5 %** (C161159).
VRAMP at 48 V (eq 38) = (19/48) x ((48 - 19) x 5u + 63.3u) x 3.97u / 820p =
0.40 V. RRAMP to VCC (eq 39) = (VCC - VRAMP) / (IOS - 25 uA) =
(9.3 - 0.4) / 38.3u = **232k** (VCC = VCCX = 9.3 V, section 4.5).
Slope ratio mc (eq 45) = 1.27-1.38 over 38-66 V; Q = 0.36-0.41: no
sub-harmonic peaking.

Overload: cycle-by-cycle at the limit; after 256 cycles in limit (1.0 ms)
the LM5116 pulls UVLO to 200 mV and restarts with a soft start (hiccup,
datasheet 6.3.3 / 6.3.7). CFT = 1 uF on UVLO/RUN, off time (eq 25)
tOFF = -(RUV1 RUV2 / (RUV1 + RUV2)) CFT ln(1 - 1.215 (RUV1 + RUV2) / (VIN RUV1)):
10.7 ms at 48 V, 6.6 ms at 60 V. D3 (BAS21) across RUV2 discharges CFT
on an input dip (datasheet 7.2.2.12 step 3).

A sustained short then averages about (1 ms + soft start) / (1 ms + 5.7 ms
+ tOFF) of the limit current: the host latches the slot on PGOOD loss
long before heat matters (ADR 0003 decision 8).

### 4.4 MOSFETs (eq 26-30)

| | part | LCSC | Rds(on) max 10 V | Qg 10 V | Qsw | Qoss | price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 high side | Infineon BSC098N10NS5 | C534398 | 9.8 mOhm | 22 nC | 7.8 nC | 30 nC | $0.91 |
| Q2 low side | Infineon BSC070N10NS5 | C534364 | 7.0 mOhm | 38 nC (26 sync) | 11 nC | 41 nC | $0.59 |

Q1 is chosen for switching charge, not Rds: at 252 kHz the high side
loses more in transitions than in conduction. HO sources ~0.8 A into the
4.8 V plateau (4.5 Ohm pull-up + 1.2 Ohm Rg at VCC 9.3 V), sinks ~1.7 A:
t_on ~10 ns, t_off ~5 ns with BSC098 (13 / 6 ns with BSC070). One BSC070 on
the low side is enough: paralleling it saves ~0.5 W of conduction but
doubles LO's charge (VCC current at start-up, section 4.5); BSC040N10NS5
(4 mOhm, 72 nC) saves 0.15 W for $0.36 more and the same start-up concern.
Estimated per-FET dissipation at 13.2 A (section 4.10): Q1 2.6 W (48 V) /
2.9 W (66 V), Q2 1.3 W / 1.5 W. Both are switching parts, not linear: SOA is
not the limit; junction temperature is (risk 3).

Gate drive current from VCC: (Qg_HS + Qg_LS) x fsw. At start-up VCC is the
internal 7.4 V regulator: ~(16 + 19) nC x 252k = 8.8 mA, inside the 15 mA
minimum regulator limit (datasheet 6.3.1, eq 29). R3 (0 Ohm, 0402) in the
HO path is the place for up to 2.2 Ohm if SW ringing needs a slower turn-on
(datasheet 6.3.8: <= 10 Ohm).

No RC snubber footprint (datasheet 7.2.2.14): there is no room for a
short snubber loop across Q2 next to the SW via field on B, and a long one
does nothing. R3 (gate resistor) is the bring-up knob for SW ringing.

### 4.5 VCC and VCCX

VCCX abs max 16 V (recommended 4.75-15 V): VOUT (19 V) cannot feed it
directly. Without VCCX the internal regulator drops VIN to 7.4 V at the
gate-drive current: (8.8 + ~5) mA x 66 V = 0.9 W in the 40.6 K/W package at
the top of the input range. With VCCX the drive current comes from VOUT at
19 V instead: the LM5116 dissipates ~0.1 W (IBIASX 1.2-1.7 mA) and the
module gains ~0.5 W.

Decision: **VCCX from VOUT through an emitter follower**, Q3 BCX56-16
(SOT-89) with a 10 V zener D2 (BZT52C10) fed by R6 10k: VCCX = 9.3 V
(8.7-9.9 V), under 16 V for any VOUT including an output overvoltage (the
zener caps it). Q3 dissipates (19 - 9.3) V x ~12 mA = 0.12 W. VCCX < VIN
always (VIN >= 35 V while switching), so the VCC-to-VIN diode
(datasheet 6.3.1) stays reverse biased. C17 (VCC) and C18 (VCCX) 1 uF.

Cost of the decision (risk 2): VCCX takes over from the internal regulator
when it passes 4.5 V, at VOUT ~5.2 V during soft start, so between VOUT 5.2 V
and ~8 V the gates see 4.5-7.4 V. Load current then is the output-cap charge
(~1.1 A) plus whatever the node draws below 8 V (its DC-ATX input normally
holds off until ~10 V). Fallback without a board change: leave Q3 off
(VCCX has an internal 100k pull-down) and accept the 0.9 W in U1.

Bootstrap: D1 BAS21 (250 V, 50 ns), C16 1 uF HB-SW (datasheet 6.3.8
recommends 1 uF; eq 21 minimum Qg / dV = 22n / 0.3 = 73 nF).

### 4.6 Output voltage (eq 24)

VOUT = 1.215 x (1 + RFB2 / RFB1), RFB2 = 36.5k 0.1 % (C852741), RFB1 = 2.49k
0.1 % (C852700): **19.03 V**. Worst case over temperature: VREF
1.195-1.231 V and 0.1 % resistors give 18.68-19.31 V (-1.8 % / +1.5 %), inside
+/-2 % (R2). With 1 % resistors it would be +/-3.2 %, hence 0.1 %. FB bias
(<= 500 nA x 2.3k) is under 1.2 mV.

### 4.7 Compensation (eq 41-48)

Type II on COMP: RCOMP = 61.9k (R11), CCOMP = 1.5 nF (C21), CHF = 22 pF
(C22). Zero 1/(2 pi RCOMP CCOMP) = 1.7 kHz, HF pole 117 kHz. The modulator
uses eq 41-45 with the real output network (3 x 100 uF / 30 mOhm polymer in
parallel with 4 x 22 uF 1210 at ~7 uF each at 19 V), the error amplifier eq
46-48 (AOL 80 dB, 3 MHz).

| VIN | load | crossover | phase margin | gain margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 38 V | 13.2 A | 19.2 kHz | 65 deg | 21 dB |
| 48 V | 13.2 A | 19.0 kHz | 64 deg | 21 dB |
| 66 V | 13.2 A | 18.9 kHz | 63 deg | 22 dB |
| 66 V | 1 A | 19.0 kHz | 62 deg | 22 dB |

Load step 6 A (node turbo): ~dI / (2 pi fc COUT) + dI ESR = 0.15 + 0.06 =
~0.2 V (1 %). Verify with a load step on the first board; a network
analyser measurement of the modulator (datasheet 7.2.2.15) is the better
check.

### 4.8 Soft start (eq 22, 23)

CSS = 47 nF (C20): tSS = 1.215 V x 47n / 10 uA = 5.7 ms. Eq 22: tSS >
VOUT COUT / (ILIM - IOUT) = 19 x 330u / (20 - 13.2) = 0.9 ms: met even into
full load. Diode emulation during soft start (start-up into a pre-biased
output), full synchronous after SS passes 3 V (RDEMB R12 = 10k to CSG,
datasheet 6.4.1). The host's PGOOD latch arms on the first PGOOD (ADR 0003),
so the soft-start time does not matter to it.

### 4.9 Input and output capacitors (eq 15, 17)

Input: 6 x 4.7 uF 100 V X7R 1210 (C381466) on B at Q1's drain and R4's GND.
At 48 V each keeps ~1.9 uF: CIN ~11 uF. RMS current IOUT sqrt(D(1-D)) =
6.5 A (1.1 A per cap). Ripple (eq 17, worst at D = 0.5) 13.2 / (4 x 252k x
11u) = 1.2 V p-p. Plus C7 1 uF 100 V at U1's VIN pin behind R2 10 Ohm.

Output: 4 x 22 uF 35 V X5R 1210 (C778721, ~7 uF each at 19 V) and 3 x 100 uF
35 V solid aluminium polymer (Yageo/Lelon class MA35V100M6X8, C46550467,
30 mOhm, 2.9 A ripple each, 6.3 x 7.7 mm). Ripple current IPP / sqrt(12) =
1.55 A at 66 V. Ripple voltage (eq 15) with ESR 10 mOhm (three polymers)
and ~330 uF: 5.4 A x 10 mOhm = ~55 mV p-p worst case before the MLCCs take
their share, < 0.3 %. The polymers are 7.7 mm tall: F side only (B limit
2.7 mm), outside the 6.5 mm screw circles.

### 4.10 Losses and efficiency (estimate)

Model: eq 26-28 and 30, Qoss and Qrr terms, body diode in the dead time,
Rds x 1.5 hot, DCR x 1.2, core loss scaled from ~0.4 W at 5 A p-p (metal dust,
no maker curve), 0.3 W board copper. At 13.2 A:

| | 38 V | 48 V | 60 V | 66 V |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 conduction / switching+Coss+Qrr | 1.33 / 1.6 W | 1.06 / 2.0 W | 0.85 / 2.5 W | 0.77 / 2.8 W |
| Q2 conduction + dead time | 1.07 W | 1.27 W | 1.43 W | 1.48 W |
| RS | 0.34 W | 0.42 W | 0.48 W | 0.50 W |
| L1 copper + core | 2.0 W | 2.2 W | 2.3 W | 2.3 W |
| gate, IC, caps, board | 0.7 W | 0.7 W | 0.7 W | 0.7 W |
| **total** | **7.2 W** | **7.7 W** | **8.3 W** | **8.6 W** |
| **efficiency** | **97.2 %** | **97.0 %** | **96.8 %** | **96.7 %** |

Treat it as +/-1 % (core loss and the switching edges are the soft
numbers). Rev A (two phases) was estimated about the same at full load; at
the node's 180 W peak B loses ~4.5 W.

### 4.11 UVLO, RUN, OVLO

- UVLO (datasheet 7.2.2.12): RUV2 R13 = 200k 1206 (> 500 x 97 V = 48.5k),
  RUV1 R14 = 6.34k. On at (1.215 - 5.4 uA x 6.15k) x 206.34 / 6.34 = **38.5 V**
  (37.0-40.0 V over the 1.170-1.262 V threshold), off ~35.2 V (0.1 V pin
  hysteresis). RUN at 60 V = 1.88 V (docs/pm-interface.md says ~1.9 V).
  UVLO at 97 V = 3.0 V (< 16 V abs max).
- RUN = the UVLO node, on J2.4: open = on; the host pulls it low through
  an open drain = off (standby: VCC up, switching off, SS discharged).
  RUN also carries CFT (1 uF): host release to switching ~ 6 ms RC plus soft
  start.
- OVLO: the LM5116 has no OVLO pin. U4 TPS3701DDCR (window comparator,
  400 mV reference, 1.8-36 V supply from VAUX): INB on R15 1M (1206) / R16
  6.04k from VIN, OUTB (open drain) on RUN. Trip 0.400 x 1006.04 / 6.04 =
  **66.6 V** rising (65.5-67.7 V with 397-403 mV and 1 % parts), back on
  ~65.8 V. C24 1 nF: 6 us filter. INA held high at VAUX, OUTA unused.
  Rev A tripped at 67.5 V; the plane slot (64.6 V nominal) still trips
  first nominally, as before (ADR 0003 decision 9 checklist).
  Above OVLO the converter does not switch, so the inductor and FETs see
  bus surges only for the comparator delay (~30 us).
- VAUX: R17 47k 0805 from VIN, D4 4.7 V zener, C26 1 uF: feeds U2, U3, U4
  (each a few uA).

### 4.12 Output supervision (unchanged from rev A)

U2 MCP65R46T-2402E/CHY: OV_KILL (J2.1) open drain low when VOUT > 2.4 x
(84.5k + 10k) / 10k = 22.68 V (R18 / R19). U3 same part: PGOOD (J2.2) high
when VOUT > 2.4 x (61.9k + 10k) / 10k = 17.26 V (R20 / R21). C27/C28 100 nF.
Independent of the LM5116 (own reference, own divider, supply from VIN).

## 6. Layout rules (the board follows these)

- Hot loop on B: C1-C6 at Q1's drain, Q1 source to Q2 drain (SW), Q2 source
  to R4, R4's GND pad back to the C1-C6 GND pads; In2 (solid GND, 0.2 mm
  under B) carries the return under the loop.
- SW: Q1/Q2 SW copper to L1 pad 1 (F) through a via field; SW kept away
  from the FB, COMP, RAMP and RT nets.
- Kelvin: CS through NT2 from R4's top (LSS) pad, CSG through NT1 from R4's
  GND pad, both net ties on the inner pad edges under R4's body, to U1
  pins 12/13; RT, CRAMP, CSS, RFB1, CCOMP return to U1 AGND
  (pin 6) side of the exposed pad.
- R8 RRAMP and C19 CRAMP at U1 pin 5; R7 at pin 3.
- VOUT feedback (R9) taken at the output caps, not at L1 pad 2.
- Screw keep-outs, aux header position, heights: docs/pm-interface.md.

## 7. Open risks and decisions to review

1. **Current limit spread** (R2): the LM5116 cycle-by-cycle threshold is 94-
   126 mV (+/-15 %), so the DC limit is 16.0-24.0 A around 19.95 A typical.
   18-20 A worst case is not reachable with this controller (rev A's LTC7810
   was +/-11 %). Measure on the first boards; RS 3.5-4.5 mOhm 2512 parts
   are stocked if the typical point has to move. At the top of the spread
   the inductor peak is ~26.6 A, at its 40 %-drop Isat.
2. **VCCX from VOUT**: gates see 4.5-7.4 V while VOUT passes 5.2-8 V in soft
   start and during an overload that sags VOUT there (hiccup ends it in
   1 ms). Fallback: do not fit Q3 (0.9 W more in U1 at 66 V).
3. **Q1 temperature**: 2.6-2.9 W in one TDSON-8. In fluid with the B copper
   and In2 under it, ~20-30 K/W gives 55-85 K rise. Measure Q1's case on
   the first board at 250 W / 60 V. If too hot: BSC070N10NS5 in parallel is
   not the fix (switching loss); lower fsw to ~200 kHz (RT 15.8k, CRAMP and
   RRAMP to recompute) or slow nothing and accept.
4. **Inductor voltage rating**: COILANK states 55 V DC absolute for the
   APS series (FEXL: 30 V, YSPIT: 30 V). Across L1: VIN - VOUT = 47.6 V at the
   66.6 V OVLO; above OVLO no switching. Same open question as rev A
   (immersion compatibility of the metal-dust coating too). The FEXL second
   source is therefore not a drop-in for the voltage rating.
5. **Single-phase input ripple**: 1.2 V p-p on the module input at full load
   (rev A, two interleaved phases, about a third of that). The plane's own
   bus caps see it; check the slot INA238 and hot-swap behaviour.
6. **ID = 22k (same as rev A)**: B behaves the same to the host (same
   ratings, OV/PGOOD thresholds, RUN). If firmware should tell B from A,
   use the reserved 47k (one resistor value, no other change).
7. **Two MCP65R46 kept** (85 in JLC stock): one TPS3701 could replace both
   with the same thresholds (dividers recomputed for 400 mV), but they
   would share one die and supply; ask before changing.
8. **No snubber footprint**: if SW rings past ~85 V, the knobs are R3 (HO
   series, up to 2.2 Ohm) and the layout of the SW loop.
9. Polymer caps in the dielectric fluid: solid electrolyte (not wet), but
   the rubber end seal is a soak-test item (R12), as for rev A's Kemet parts.
10. Thermal/efficiency numbers are estimates without maker core-loss data.

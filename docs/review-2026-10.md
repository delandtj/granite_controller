# Design review 2026-10 (rev C, branch eight-node-expander)

Three verification passes before the first JLC order, run 2026-10-08:
(1) KiCad MCP validation suite, (2) datasheet review of the critical
circuits, (3) JLC DFM. Nothing in the schematic or board was changed;
this is the finding list. Fixes happen on request.

Baseline at review time: `kicad-cli sch erc --severity-all` = 0;
`kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all --refill-zones` =
0 unconnected, 0 parity, 4 violations (2 silk_edge_clearance at the
WROOM antenna overhang, 2 starved_thermal on J8 GND pins 3/4).

Bus assumption for the power checks: 19.5 V +-5 % (18.5-20.5 V).

Summary: 1 blocker, 12 should-fix, the rest notes.

## Blocker

### B1. U15 GPA7 is output-only: node 8 sense cannot be read

- Evidence: MCP23017 datasheet DS20001952D (current, LCSC C47023 copy),
  p.1 and Table 1-1: "GPA7, GPB7 are output only (MCP23017)". Rev C
  of the datasheet did not say this. U15 pin 28 (GPA7) = NODE_ON8.
- Change: move NODE_ON8 to a free U15 input (GPB4, pin 5). Leave GPB7
  unused. Note for J8 add-on designs: GPx7 is output-only.
  U14 is unaffected (all outputs).

## Should-fix

### S1. F3 input PTC too small for the worst-case load

- Evidence: 1206L050/33NR (LUTE C19193043) hold 0.50 A at 25 C,
  0.42 A at 40 C, 0.35 A at 60 C; trip 1.0 A. Worst-case board draw is
  ~0.41 A from 19 V (docs/controller.md; 1.2 A at 5.2 V, 88 %). A J8
  load just under F2's trip (~0.84 A at 40 C) pulls ~0.5 A from 19 V,
  so F3 trips before F2 and the whole controller drops off instead of
  just the header. In fluid, hold/trip rise with the better cooling.
- Change: F3 to a >= 0.75 A hold, >= 24 V PTC (1206L075 / 1210L110
  class; check JLC stock), or lower F2 to ~0.35 A hold.

### S2. VBUS floats up through D3 leakage: USB console may not attach

- Evidence: VBUS net = D3 anode, J2, U4 pin 5 only. SS34 (MDD C8678)
  IR up to 0.5 mA at 25 C (datasheet Fig. 2). On 19 V with no cable,
  VBUS is pulled toward +5V; a USB-C source waits for VBUS < vSafe0V
  (0.8 V) before attaching.
- Change: 4.7k VBUS -> GND plus 1-4.7 uF on VBUS (<= 10 uF).
- Related note: C2 220 uF behind D3 exceeds the USB 10 uF inrush limit
  when powered from USB. Bench only; hosts tolerate it.

### S3. Header 5 V sags to ~4.4 V

- Evidence: BUCK_5V 5.215 V minus SS34 D2 (0.33 V at 0.3 A, 0.45 V at
  1.2 A, MDD Fig. 3) minus F2 (0.15-0.75 R x 0.5 A).
- Change: R38 13k -> 12.4k: 0.6 x (1 + 100/12.4) = 5.44 V. +5V then
  ~5.0 V loaded, <= ~5.4 V light, below TLV62569 5.5 V recommended max
  (6 V abs, datasheet 6.1/6.3).

### S4. TLP176AM LED current below Toshiba's recommended minimum

- Evidence: TLP176AM section 7: IF 5 / 7.5 / 25 mA (min/typ/max), IFT
  max 3 mA. With 330R (R101-R116): typ ~5.5 mA, worst case (3.2 V
  rail, VF 1.35 V, MCP23017 VOH drop 0.7 V) ~3.5 mA.
- Change: 270R: typ ~6.5 mA, worst ~4 mA, all 16 on ~104 mA < 125 mA
  MCP23017 VDD limit. Not 220R (16 x 8 mA = 128 mA).

### S5. ESP32 EN RC and module decoupling far from U1

- Evidence: C14 (1u) ~38 mm from U1 EN pin 3; C6_EN trace 91.6 mm.
  C12 (10u) / C13 (100n) ~38 mm from 3V3 pin 2. ESP32-C6 HW design
  guidelines 1.3.3 (keep CHIP_PU short, spurious resets); WROOM-1
  datasheet section 9 (22u + 0.1u at the pin).
- Change: move R7, C14, C12, C13 next to pins 2/3; TP1 may stay
  remote behind the RC. Lower priority, same issue: W5500 VDD pin 28
  (nearest cap C11 at 12.4 mm), U15 VDD (26 mm).

### S6. VIN_SENSE filter cap at the wrong end

- Evidence: C51 at the divider (x ~44); ADC pin 5 at x 123.75 after
  129 mm of high-impedance trace, partly under the module and along
  y 78.0 (0.6 mm from the edge). C6 datasheet 5.5 specifies ADC
  accuracy with 100 nF at the pin.
- Change: move C51 (ideally R63/R64 too) to U1 pin 5.

### S7. Y2 load caps slightly heavy

- Evidence: Y2 X322525MOB4SI: CL 12 pF, C0 <= 3 pF. C26/C27 18 pF give
  9 pF + 3-5 pF stray = 12-14 pF, pulling ~0 to -20 ppm; with +-10 ppm
  tolerance and +-20 ppm temperature it approaches the +-50 ppm 802.3
  limit. docs/controller.md "CL 18 pF" is the W5500 generic figure,
  stale for this crystal.
- Change: C26/C27 -> 15 pF; fix the doc.

### S8. Hole-clearance custom rule is dead (unitless value)

- Evidence: granite_controller.kicad_dru `(constraint hole_clearance
  (min 0.25))` has no unit and KiCad 10 ignores it; with `0.25mm` the
  same rule reports 30 violations. GND/+3V3 zones sit 0.1505 mm from
  NPTHs on all layers at H1-H4, the J1 pegs (63.84, 40.04 and its
  pair) and the J2 pegs (96.61/102.39, 34.88). JLC NPTH-to-copper
  min is 0.2.
- Change: `(min 0.3mm)`, refill, re-run DRC. Optionally a 6 mm copper
  keep-out ring on F/B.Cu at H1-H4 if the M3 heads must not touch GND.

### S9. Assembly panel: edge clearance and overhangs

- Evidence: U1 overhangs the bottom edge by 6.55 mm (courtyard to
  y 85.1, edge 78.6); J2 overhangs the top edge 0.55 mm, J1 flush.
  C4 0.25 mm and L1 0.75 mm from the left edge; JST row 0.45-0.75 mm
  from both long edges. JLC wants 3-5 mm free edge for SMT and adds
  rails; V-score needs 0.4 mm copper-to-score (zones are 0.299 mm,
  vias 0.35 mm, e.g. (200, 29.2), (241.5, 29.2)).
- Change: order with mouse-bite rails and cut-outs for U1/J1/J2
  (agree with JLC) or panelize ourselves. If V-cut, pull copper back
  to 0.5 mm.

### S10. CPL rotations to verify in JLC's preview

- Evidence: tools/jlcfab.py applies -90 to every `^SOT-23` (U2, U4,
  U8) and 180 to the whole `^CP_EIA-` family, wider than the community
  table (3216/3528 only). That includes C2, a polarized polymer
  capacitor.
- Change: check in JLC's preview, polarity first: C2; D1, D2, D3, D8
  (SMA), D6, D7 (SOD-123F), D5 (SOD-923), D4 LED; Y2 (90 deg error puts
  the crystal on its GND pads); U1 (old table had 270 for ESP32-W*);
  RN1/RN2; U3 LQFP; U6, U7, U18 (USON). THT connectors are placed at
  pad centre; JST bodies sit ~1.5 mm off the pad row.

## Notes

Power:

- N1. L3 FXL0630-3R3-M is rated 30 V withstand; ~30 V across it at
  the TVS clamp. FXL0630-3R3-MV75 (75 V) if JLC stocks it.
- N2. TVS D1 SMAJ22A: VRWM 22 V (1.5 V over a 20.5 V bus), VC 35.5 V
  at 11.3 A vs LMR51430 36 V recommended / 38 V abs max. Thin but OK.
  A 24-28 V supply mix-up makes D1 conduct until F3 trips. Keep.
- N3. Nothing protects +5V if U8's high-side FET shorts (19 V onto the
  TLV62569, 6 V abs max, and onto VBUS through D2). Option: 5-6 V SMA
  TVS on BUCK_5V as a crowbar so F3 trips.
- N4. D6 SMF3.3A leaks up to 800 uA at 3.3 V (rail 3.315 V); ~2.6 mW,
  harmless. ESD only (VC 7.3 V). Value field "SMF3.3" vs MPN
  "SMF3.3A" (cosmetic). D7 SMF5.0A fine (VBR min 6.4 V).
- N5. L1 SWPA3015S2R2 Isat 1.6 A: the 3V3 rail is good for ~1.3 A
  total, not 2 A as the doc suggests (F1 trips at 0.75 A anyway).
- N6. From USB the worst-case 1.2 A exceeds USB 2.0 / Type-C default
  current. Bench only; document.
- N7. C40/C41 (Fenghua 1206 4.7u/50V X7R) have no DC-bias curve; by
  comparison ~2.3-2.6 uF each at 19 V, ~5 uF total, meets TI 9.2.2.6.
  C44/C45 ~11-13 uF each at 5.2 V. Estimates, not vendor data.

MCU / Ethernet / IO:

- N8. F.Cu under the module body (x 107-123, y 59.1-78.6): VIN_SENSE
  18.4 mm, LED_HB 15.1, I2C_INT_SDA 15.0, I2C_INT_SCL 12.6, OW_MCU
  12.4, GND 11.7, I2C_EXT_SCL 8.9, GPIO_IO8 7.8, I2C_EXT_SDA 4.4, +3V3
  3.5, plus 10 non-GND vias (tented). The I2C_EXT_SDA via at
  (118.7, 71.1) is ~0.25 mm from the EPAD land edge: move at least
  that one. Espressif has no explicit ban.
- N9. Firmware: INTA is push-pull after reset (IOCON.ODR = 0) and
  EXP_INT is shared: set ODR = 1 on every expander before enabling
  GPINTEN. U15's dry contacts are on port B and INTB is not wired:
  set IOCON.MIRROR = 1.
- N10. The U14 fail-safe covers reset and high-Z only; firmware that
  hangs with GPIO10 high keeps the relays live (watchdog). Reword the
  "hung -> released" line in docs/controller.md.
- N11. 1-wire is on U0TXD/U0RXD (GPIO16/17): ROM/bootloader logs go
  onto the probe bus (harmless, longest low ~78 us). Firmware moves
  the console to USB-Serial-JTAG. D5 is one ESD diode for J4-J7,
  6-25 mm away.
- N12. Node sense (open item): IF = (V_PLED - 1.2) / (220 + R_mb);
  ~2 mA needed with the 10k pull-up (GB rank CTR min 30 % at 1 mA).
  Works up to R_mb ~830R at 3.3 V PLED+, ~1.68k at 5 V. Option: R39-R46
  to 47k, needs IF ~0.3 mA, roughly 4x the range.
- N13. External I2C: 4.7k with 8 x LTC4282, add-ons and cable
  (~200 pF) rise ~800 ns: run it at 100 kHz, not 400.
- N14. W5500 SCLK guaranteed to 33.3 MHz (datasheet 5.5.4 note 5);
  docs/controller.md says 20-40 MHz. Cap at 33 MHz.
- N15. HR911105A is rated 0-70 C; relevant if the fluid runs hot.

DFM / board:

- N16. Silk: 116 texts at 0.8/0.15 mm; JLC now lists 1.0 mm min
  height. 990 footprint-library lines at 0.12 mm (JLC 0.15). Silk
  within 0.10 mm of pads (e.g. R13); Gerbers subtract mask so none
  prints on pads. J8 is DNP but its silk prints.
- N17. Three 0.5/0.2 GND vias 0.404 mm from U1 pad 29's in-pad holes
  ((117.80, 70.11), (118.00, 71.40), (116.50, 69.90)): same net, legal,
  redundant; delete. U1.29 in-pad 0.3 mm holes are open: some solder
  wicking, accept or pay for filled vias.
- N18. The Gerber zip includes drill-map .gbr files; drop them from
  tools/jlcfab.py output.
- N19. ETH_TXN/ETH_RXN each ~3 mm on B.Cu with 2 vias (polarity
  crossovers). Negligible at 100BASE-TX.
- N20. The board is 250 x 50 mm (Edge.Cuts x 25..275, y 28.6..78.6),
  not 230 x 40 as docs/handoff.md says. Update the docs.
- N21. Impedance (2D field solve, JLC04161H-7628, outer prepreg
  0.2104 mm Er 4.4, mask 15 um Er 3.8): ETH 0.16/0.15 on F.Cu over In1
  ~107R air / ~98R fluid with mask / ~101R fluid no mask; USB
  0.25/0.15 ~91R air / ~84R fluid. Both on target for their medium.
  USB_CONN_D+/- 17-19 mm at 0.2 mm, irrelevant at 12 Mbit/s. Worth a
  quick confirmation in JLC's web calculator.

## Checked OK (key numbers)

- U8 LMR51430: pinout matches; Vref 0.591/0.600/0.609 V (7.5); Vout
  5.215 V; fsw 0.95-1.25 MHz, on-time ~249 ns > 70 ns min; L3 ripple
  1.04 A pk-pk (K 35 %), Isat 8.7 A; C43 100n/50V; EN to VIN allowed
  (8.3.3), UVLO 3.89-4.5 V.
- D8/D2 SS34 40 V > 35.5 V clamp. Reverse polarity: D1 forward, F3
  trips.
- U2 TLV62569: Vref 0.6 V, Vout 3.315 V; 2.2 uH + 22 uF is a proven
  cell (Table 4); dropout ~0.11 V, runs from USB.
- F1 0805L035/24XR, F2 1206L050/15YR voltage ratings OK; TPD4E05 VRWM
  5.5 V; 330R + 4.7k adds ~0.23 V to I2C VOL.
- USB-C CC 5.1k 1 %; USBLC6-2SC6 pinout (from memory, ST PDF not
  fetched); R34/R35 22R 2.3 mm from U1.
- ESP32-C6 straps: GPIO9 internal pull-up + TP2; GPIO8 R27 pull-up;
  GPIO15 ignored with default eFuses (do not burn JTAG_SEL_ENABLE);
  MTMS R19 pull-down, MTDI ~1.7 V (SDIO edge straps only). EN 10k/1u
  (10 ms) per Espressif.
- Antenna fully off-board, feed flush at the edge; zones stop at
  y 78.3-78.6.
- W5500 network identical to the WIZnet shield (33R, 49R9 TX pull-ups
  to 3V3A, RX 49R9 to RCT + 6.8 nF, RCT 10n, TCT 10R + 22n, EXRES
  12.4k 1 %, TOCAP 4.7u, 1V2O 10n, 1M across XI/XO, PMODE open = all
  capable). HR911105A pin map and LED polarity correct. L2 drop < 7 mV.
- Addresses U14 0x20, U15 0x21, TMP1075 0x48. U14/U15 RESET pulled
  down by R32. Internal I2C ~160 ns rise, fine at 400 kHz.
- VIN_SENSE 100k/10k: 19 V -> 1.73 V, 35.5 V clamp -> 3.23 V (ATTEN3).
- DFM minima vs JLC: track 0.16 (0.09); clearance 0.15 outer, 0.2
  inner (0.09); vias 0.5/0.2 and 0.6/0.3, ring 0.15 (0.15); hole to
  hole 0.50 via, 0.58 PTH; PTH to other-net copper 0.43 (0.28);
  copper to edge 0.299 (0.2); mask web 0.15 (0.10); J2 slots 0.6
  (0.35); Gerber layer set complete, Excellon metric; BOM/CPL cover
  all 204 footprints, every placed part has LCSC.
- KiCad MCP suite: no real defects; the FAILs are root-sheet-only
  parsing (0 symbols seen), NC pins U14:11/14, pull-ups behind the
  RN1 330R, FB/SW nets flagged as rails, and 45 right-angle corners
  (cosmetic).

## Proposed fix order

1. B1, S8 (schematic pin swap; one-line rule fix + refill).
2. S1-S4, S7 (value/part changes, no layout impact beyond S2's two
   new parts).
3. S5, S6, N8, N17 (layout moves around U1).
4. S9, S10 at order time with JLC.
